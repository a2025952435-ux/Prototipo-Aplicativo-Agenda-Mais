name: AgendaMais+ demo scope
description: Scope and data-handling boundaries for the AgendaMais+ academic prototype.
---

Keep AgendaMais+ as a clearly labeled academic demonstration unless the user explicitly changes scope. Do not add real authentication, medical services or integrations, real provider availability, video visits, or speech synthesis. Demo appointments, notifications, and accessibility preferences may persist locally in the browser; never persist passwords or identifying profile fields.

**Why:** The user chose to expand the prototype to 22 screens while keeping data and audio demonstrative. Local-only sample state preserves the demo flow without turning it into a real medical service.

**How to apply:** Keep demo notices visible, distinguish fictional data from real availability, and keep credentials and personal profile fields out of storage and network requests.
